Quest for Paws Animal Rescue and Sanctuary is a nonprofit organization dedicated to rescuing, fostering, and rehoming abandoned animals.

How We Help

Three ways your support changes pets’ lives

Animal Rescue

We go on quests to rescue and recover any dog or cat roaming our streets.

Veterinary Services

We provide free veterinary treatment to strays, rescues, and pets.

Fostering and Adoption

We help our rescues find their temporary and forever homes.
